H-2 near Racha to Sveti Trojca Arhierei

H-2 is one of Galičica National Park's easier and shorter hiking trails. It runs 1.1 km from near Racha to Sveti Trojca Arhierei, climbing about 138 m and descending 0 m in the direction drawn, between 765 m and 914 m. In Macedonian: Рача, Свети Тројца Архиереи.

About this line

The line is from the park's own trail map as archived in October 2020. It was drawn by hand on a map, not recorded on the ground, so expect it to be off by 20 to 50 m in places. Most of the park's trails are marked on the ground with paint blazes and numbered guideposts, but markings fade and paths get overgrown. Carry an offline map and turn back if the path disappears.
