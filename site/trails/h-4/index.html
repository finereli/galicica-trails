H-4 the MTB-3 junction to the T-1 junction

H-4 is one of Galičica National Park's easier and shorter hiking trails. It's 5.4 km from the MTB-3 junction to the T-1 junction, with about 46 m of climbing and 107 m of descent in the direction drawn, between 1,419 m and 1,498 m.

About this line

The line is from the park's own trail map as archived in October 2020. It was drawn by hand on a map, not recorded on the ground, so expect it to be off by 20 to 50 m in places. Most of the park's trails are marked on the ground with paint blazes and numbered guideposts, but markings fade and paths get overgrown. Carry an offline map and turn back if you lose the path.
