G-6 Galičica pass (Lipova Livada) to Magaro peak

G-6 is one of Galičica National Park's high ridge trails above 1,600 m. It runs 5.2 km from Galičica pass (Lipova Livada) to Magaro peak, climbing about 670 m and descending 321 m in the direction drawn, between 1,567 m and 2,247 m. In Macedonian: превој Галичица, Липова Ливада.

About this line

G-6 climbs from the pass through beech forest, then loops over Magaro (2,254 m) and back down to the same path, so the GPX has two pieces: the approach and the loop. The line is from OpenStreetMap (relation 7330532). The park's own map drew the climb from the pass as a straight 1.4 km line, so we used the mapped path instead. Most of the park's trails are marked on the ground with paint blazes and numbered guideposts, but markings fade and paths get overgrown. Carry an offline map and turn back if the path disappears.
