MTB-9 St Cyril and Methodius Church to Konjsko

MTB-9 is one of Galičica National Park's mountain bike trails, some of them handy links between hiking routes. It's 8.3 km from St Cyril and Methodius Church to Konjsko, with about 203 m of climbing and 184 m of descent in the direction drawn, between 846 m and 1,020 m. In Macedonian: Св. Кирил и Методиј, Коњско.

About this line

The line is from the park's own trail map as archived in October 2020. It was drawn by hand on a map, not recorded on the ground, so expect it to be off by 20 to 50 m in places. Most of the park's trails are marked on the ground with paint blazes and numbered guideposts, but markings fade and paths get overgrown. Carry an offline map and turn back if you lose the path.
