Ramne - Letnica - Visok Vrv - Krstec - Velestovo

One of the ten day routes suggested by Galičica National Park, made up of G-1, T-3, T-2, H-4. Start at 990 m, finish at 1,080 m.

What the park says

A mostly west-facing trail with the climbing spread fairly evenly. Parts are in low vegetation, higher parts in forest, and the highest stretch is open ground with wide views. The ground is hard and stony, grassy in places. In several places, especially below Letnica and at Skala, you cross streams that are easy to cross for most of the year.
