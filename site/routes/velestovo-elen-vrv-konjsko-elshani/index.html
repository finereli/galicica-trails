Velestovo - Elen Vrv - Konjsko - Elshani

One of the ten day routes suggested by Galičica National Park, made up of T-4, H-5, G-3. Start at 1,080 m, finish at 900 m.

What the park says

A west-facing trail with the climbing spread fairly evenly. The ground is hard and stony, grassy in places.

Original text in Macedonian

Западно експонирана патека. Распоредот на искачувањата е релативно равномерен. Подлогата е тврда, каменита, а на места се поминува низ тревни делови.

Велестово – Елен Врв – Коњско – Елшани

Trails on this route

  • T-4 near Velestovo to above Leskoec (1,508 m), 14.0 km
  • H-5 Elshani to near Velestovo, 6.9 km
  • G-3 the H-5 junction to near Shipokno, 0.8 km

In the GPX each trail is a separate track, drawn in full. Some trails go on past the point where you leave them for the next one, so the tracks add up to more than the park's route length. Go by the trailheads and the park's distance.
