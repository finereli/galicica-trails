Every marked trail in Galičica National Park

All 38 numbered trails on the mountain between Lake Ohrid and Lake Prespa, with a map, an elevation profile and a free GPX file for each. The park's own trail map disappeared from its website; this is that map, rescued from a 2020 archive and checked against OpenStreetMap.

Galičica (Галичица) is the limestone mountain between Ohrid and Prespa, a national park since 1958. The park marks its trails with codes you'll see painted on the signposts: H for the easier ones, T for the longer and steeper, G for the ridges above 1,600 m, and MTB for bike trails. The codes on this site match the signs.

Сите обележани патеки на Националниот парк Галичица (H, T, G и MTB), со карта, висински профил и GPX за секоја патека. Податоците се од картата на патеки на Паркот од 2020 година.

The trails

Length and climb are measured on the line as the park drew it, from one end to the other. Climb is from SRTM elevation data, so treat it as a good estimate rather than gospel. Tap a trail for its map, profile, guideposts and connections.
